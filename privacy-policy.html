2. What we process, and why

Your account

Your e-mail address and password (stored only as a one-way hash), whether your e-mail address is confirmed, the short-lived codes we e-mail you to confirm it, reset your password or change your address (valid for 30 minutes), when your account was created and when it was last active, and your sign-in sessions (each valid for up to 90 days). If you sign in with Apple or Google, we also keep the identifier Apple or Google gives your account, and use the e-mail address it shares to create or link your account. We use this to create your account, sign you in and keep it secure — performance of our contract with you (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Your training

What you plan and log: workouts and their sessions (dates, times, durations, exercises, sets, weights, repetitions, effort and notes), workout plans, training blocks, your own exercises and preferences about exercises, personal records, goals and weekly targets, and the app’s settings (units, language, appearance, notification choices, training goal, experience, equipment). Optionally, the body measurements you enter — bodyweight, sex, height and age — which the app uses for statistics and strength standards, and the bodyweight it records with each session. Your phone keeps all of it first, and works without a connection; our server keeps a copy, as a backup and to sync it between your devices.

  • We use your training to provide Workouts to you: to store, back up and sync it, and to calculate your statistics — performance of our contract (Article 6(1)(b)).
  • Your body measurements, and training data in so far as they reveal something about your health, are processed on the basis of your explicit consent (Articles 6(1)(a) and 9(2)(a)), which you give by entering them and can withdraw at any time by removing them in the app or deleting your account.
  • To sync, we record which of your devices sync, by a random identifier the app creates when it is installed, with its platform and when it last synced — and, on the Free plan, which one is backed up and when you last moved the backup.
  • When you delete something in the app, our server keeps it marked as deleted, so that your other devices delete it too; it is erased with your account.

The AI Coach

If you use the AI Coach and have agreed to its notice in the app, then each time you ask it something — and once a week, if your plan includes weekly check-ins and you turn them on — we send to our AI provider your message, the conversation so far, the training data the coach reads to answer you (for example your workouts, sets, records, targets, plans and the names and notes you gave them, and strength estimates calculated from your bodyweight and sex), and where you are in the app (the screen, the date, your language and units, your weekly goal, training goal and experience). We do not send your name, your e-mail address or your raw body measurements; each request carries a pseudonymous identifier instead. Your conversations are kept on your account and synced to your devices; with each one, our server also keeps what the coach read to answer you. We use this to give you the coach you asked for — performance of our contract and, for data about your health, your explicit consent, given when you agree to the notice.

For each request we also record its cost and size, and whether it drafted a training programme, without its content. Where the coach is counted in credits, we keep your account’s credits: each grant of them — a month of your subscription, a free trial, a top-up you bought, or credits we gave you, with our reason — when it began and when it expires, what is left of it, whether it was taken back (for example after a refund), and what each request used of it. We use this to keep the coach within your allowance and its costs, and to show you in the app (Settings → Subscription → AI Coach) what you have left and what used it — performance of our contract.

Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions — and the AI Coach credit top-ups, where the app offers them — are sold by Apple through the App Store and by Google through Google Play. They take your payment and handle refunds; we never see your card or your billing address. Apple and Google keep their own records of your purchases under their own privacy policies. To know what your account has bought, we use RevenueCat, which receives the stores’ records of your purchases — the product, its dates, its price and currency, your country and the store’s transaction identifiers — under a random identifier we give your account, never your e-mail address.

We keep your plan, whether it is a free trial, the store and product you bought it with, when its current period began and ends and whether it has been paid for, whether it renews and whether a payment failed, the store’s transaction identifier of that period and of each top-up you bought, and the stores’ notifications about your purchases. If we give your account a plan at no charge — for example to the accounts that already existed when the paid plans began, as founding members — we record it, with the reason and, where it was given in our administration console, who gave it. We use this to give your account the plan and the credits it paid for, and to take back what a refunded purchase brought — performance of our contract — and to see how our subscriptions sell — our legitimate interest in running our business (Article 6(1)(f)).

How the app is used, and when it fails

  • Usage events. The app records events about how it is used — for example that a workout was finished, with its duration and how many exercises and sets it had, or that the plans screen was opened and how it was closed — with your account, a random installation identifier, the app’s version and your platform. They never contain your notes, names you typed or your body measurements. We use them to understand and improve Workouts — our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Crash reports. When the app fails, it sends us what went wrong: the error and where it happened, the last steps before it (screens opened, requests made, sync events), the app’s version, your platform, system version and device model, the installation identifier and, if you are signed in, your account. On Android, a crash of the app’s native code is reported by Firebase Crashlytics (section 4). We use crash reports to fix errors — our legitimate interest.
  • Problem reports. When you send one from Settings → Diagnostics, we receive what you wrote, your device and app versions, the state of sync, a summary of the app’s recent errors and — unless you switch it off before sending — the app’s recent log, which the app shows you first. What you wrote, with your device and app details and your account, also reaches our support inbox by e-mail. We use it to answer you and fix the problem.
  • Server logs. Our server and the network in front of it record every request: your IP address, the time, the address requested, the answer and how long it took, your device’s user agent and, while you are signed in, your account. We use them to keep the service running and secure — our legitimate interest.

E-mails

We e-mail you only about your account: codes to confirm your address, reset your password or change your address, and a notice when you ask to delete your account — performance of our contract. We do not send marketing e-mail.

Notifications

Reminders — including the one before a free trial ends — the rest timer and the workout in progress on your lock screen are notifications the app shows on your phone, with your phone’s permission; we do not send them from our server. Your notification choices are part of your settings.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access your data and receive a copy of it, to have it corrected, deleted or restricted, to take it with you, and to object to what we do on the basis of our legitimate interest. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time, without affecting what was done before. In the app, you can export your training (Settings → Manage your account → Export my training), correct it, and delete your account; for anything else, write to support@wkubasik.pl. We answer within one month. You can also complain to a data protection authority — in Poland, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa) — or to the authority where you live or work.

7. Other things you should know

  • We make no decisions about you by automated means that have legal or similarly significant effects. The AI Coach makes suggestions; you decide what to do with them.
  • Providing your data is voluntary, but without an e-mail address (or Sign in with Apple or Google) you cannot have an account, and without your training data the app has nothing to show you.
  • Workouts is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.
  • Everything travels to and from our server encrypted (HTTPS), passwords are stored only as one-way hashes, and access to our server is limited to those who run it. On your phone, your data is protected by the phone’s own security, such as its lock screen.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version here with its date and, if the change matters to you, tell you in the app or by e-mail before it takes effect.
