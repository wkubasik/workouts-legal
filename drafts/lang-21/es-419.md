# es-419 legal site: notes for the reviewer (LANG-21)

> The lawyer's briefing for this language's draft of the legal site (workouts-frontend `docs/languages-plan.md`, LANG-21,
> §Q.2), written as it was drafted on 25 October 2026. Not published: `.assetsignore` keeps `drafts/` and every `*.md`
> out of the upload. Once the language is published, its As built records the sign-off and this file goes.

The source is the English sections of `workouts-legal` after LANG-21's restructuring of the pages, which changed only the wrappers, so the
content matches what was translated. Polish was used only to check meaning. One Spanish text serves Latin America and
Spain. It uses *tú* and *ustedes*, never *vos* or *vosotros*, and the app's own words from `es-419.json`.

## Terms chosen

- **Title of the Terms: *Términos de uso*.** The app links to the page under that name (`settingsPage.legal.termsOfUse`,
  `paywall.termsOfUse`), so the link and the page agree. The footer and every link use it. In prose the Terms are
  *estos términos*. The Privacy Policy is *Política de privacidad*.
- **GDPR wording.** The regulation is the RGPD and articles are cited as *artículo 6.1.b) del RGPD*.
  - The English names the GDPR only once. Spanish adds *del RGPD* to every citation, so a bare "(artículo 6.1.b))" does
    not end in two closing brackets.
  - Terms follow the RGPD's Spanish text: *responsable del tratamiento*, *tratar*/*tratamiento*, *ejecución de un
    contrato*, *interés legítimo*, *consentimiento explícito*, *autoridad de control*.
  - The rights are rectification, *supresión*, *limitación del tratamiento*, *portabilidad* and *oposición*. Automated
    decisions take Art. 22's *efectos jurídicos … le afecten significativamente de modo similar*.
  - Also: *cláusulas contractuales tipo*; *Marco de Privacidad de Datos UE-EE. UU.*, with the English name in brackets
    as Polish does; *Espacio Económico Europeo*.
  - UODO: *el Presidente de la Oficina de Protección de Datos Personales (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych …)*.
- **Consumer law.**
  - *reclamación* for a complaint. This is Spain's legal term and is understood in Latin America, where people also say
    *reclamo*.
  - *derecho de desistimiento*, *normas imperativas*, *de forma extrajudicial*.
  - *rzecznik konsumentów*: *defensor de los consumidores municipal o de distrito*.
  - *Inspekcja Handlowa*: *un inspectorado provincial de la Inspección de Comercio*.
- **Consumer, kept gender-neutral (translation guide §4.2).** *Si tienes la condición de consumidor* and *tus derechos de
  consumidor*, not *si eres consumidor* or *como consumidor*. The reviewer may prefer the plainer form.
  *Pregnant* became *en caso de embarazo*.
- **The business.**
  - *trading as*: *que opera con el nombre comercial*.
  - Sole proprietorship: *empresa individual*.
  - Business name: *Nombre comercial*. Registered address: *Domicilio registrado*.
  - NIP: *número de identificación fiscal*.
  - Podkarpackie Voivodeship: *Voivodato de Subcarpacia*.
- **App words.**
  - Body measurements: *datos corporales* (the Settings page *Datos corporales*).
  - Your own exercises: *ejercicios personalizados*.
  - Allowance: *límite de uso*, as the app's quota card says (`ai.quota.budget.title`).
  - Top-up: *recarga*. A grant of credits: *asignación*. Credits taken back: *se retiró*.
  - Weekly check-ins: *revisiones semanales*. The AI Coach's notice: *aviso*.
  - Problem report: *reporte*, the app's word. Logs: *registros*.
- **No app word existed for these.**
  - Crash report: *reporte de fallos*. *Fallo* is Spain's noun and *falla* Latin America's. The verb (*la app falla*)
    is the same in both.
  - One-way hash: *hash unidireccional*. Pseudonymous identifier: *identificador seudónimo*.
  - User agent: *agente de usuario (user agent)*. *Costo* (Spain also says *coste*). *Periodo*, as §5.11 spells it.
- **Labels quoted, all checked in `es-419.json`.**
  - *Configuración → Administrar tu cuenta → Eliminar cuenta* and *Exportar mis entrenamientos*. The profile card on the
    settings hub reads *Administrar tu cuenta*.
  - *Configuración → Diagnóstico → Reportar un problema*.
  - *Configuración → Workouts Pro → Administrar suscripción*, *Restaurar compras* and the *Entrenador IA* card on the
    subscription page (LANG-44); on the Free plan the row reads *Mejorar plan*.
  - Plans: *Gratis*, *Pro*, *Pro + Entrenador IA*; *el plan Gratis* after a preposition.
- **Sign in with Apple / Google.** Written descriptively (*con tu cuenta de Apple o de Google*, *iniciar sesión con Apple
  o Google*). The app's buttons say *Continuar con Apple/Google*, and Apple's Spanish product name varies.
- **Terms §5, "withdraw this agreement".** Written as *retirar este consentimiento*, to match the Privacy Policy, which
  bases this on consent (Art. 6.1.a).
- **The deletion page.**
  - Title: *Eliminar tu cuenta y tus datos*, and so do its links, the index's button and the footer (LANG-44: one name
    for the page).
  - Support page: *Soporte*. Company page: *Información de la empresa*, footer *Empresa*. Home: *Inicio*.
- **mailto subjects.** *Eliminar mi cuenta de Workouts*, *Eliminar parte de mis datos de Workouts*, *Soporte de
  Workouts*. They are ASCII, so `%20` is the only encoding. The example requests on the deletion page use *ustedes*
  (*“borren todas mis sesiones…”*).
- **`ui.json`.** The draft notice says *pendiente de revisión jurídica*, which avoids a gendered *un abogado*.
- **Index title.** The index's English section carries `data-title="Workouts — Legal"`, set when the drafts went onto the
  pages: *Workouts — Información legal*.

## Adapted rather than translated

- **iPhone path.** *Configuración (o Ajustes) → tu nombre → Suscripciones*. iOS says *Configuración* in Latin American
  Spanish and *Ajustes* in Spain's.
- **Play Store path.** *en la app Play Store, en tu perfil → Pagos y suscripciones → Suscripciones*. The `&` becomes
  *y*, as Play writes it in Spanish.
- **Support intro.** "found a bug" became *¿quieres reportar un error…?*, which avoids the preterite/perfect split
  between Latin America and Spain. *e.g.* became *p. ej.*.
- **Privacy §2, "Your phone … works without a connection".** Written as *la app funciona sin conexión*, following the
  Polish.

## Store and OS labels I am not sure of

- **Apple's EULA.** I don't know its official Spanish title. I wrote *Contrato de licencia de usuario final de
  aplicaciones con licencia* with the English name in brackets.
- **Google Play's terms.** *Condiciones de Servicio de Google Play* is likely right but not checked.
- **iOS and Play paths.** I am confident of *Suscripciones* and *Pagos y suscripciones*. The Play profile entry is
  described, not quoted.

## Problems found in the source

**LANG-44 fixed these on 26 October 2026, in English, Polish and this draft**, as the product owner decided
(workouts-frontend `docs/languages-plan.md`, LANG-44): the pages name the Settings row as it reads — *Configuración →
Workouts Pro*, or *Mejorar plan* on the Free plan — and the page it opens as the subscription page; English's site calls
the Terms *Terms of Use*, as the app does; the deletion page has one name in every language, its title (*Eliminar tu cuenta y tus datos*), in
its links, the index and the footer; problem reports name their bases — answering one, by e-mail too, is *ejecución de nuestro contrato*,
finding and fixing the problem *interés legítimo*; the bullet names the switch, *Adjuntar actividad reciente*, and what it attaches, a log of the
app's recent activity; the Android app no longer carries Firebase Cloud Messaging, which nothing used, so §4 names
Crashlytics alone; the introduction says who else processes the data rather than who processes it *for us*, and §4
opens on those who process it rather than on companies, since it lists controllers and the authorities too; the
purchases paragraph gives the account the credits it *bought or was given*; the quotes are typographic; and the Polish
addresses the reader neutrally (*usunąłem* stays: the person's own words). The Terms' and the Privacy Policy's "Last
updated" read 26 October 2026 in every language. Checked and left as they are: the index naming the developer and the
other pages his business, both true; "goals and weekly targets", a description rather than a label; and "plan" for
the tier and the training plan alike in English, which this draft tells apart. Each item below is as it was found.

1. **Two names for the Terms.** The site calls them "Terms of Service" (page title, footer, links). The app calls them
   "Terms of Use" (`settingsPage.legal.termsOfUse`, `paywall.termsOfUse`), so someone tapping "Terms of Use" lands on
   "Terms of Service". Spanish follows the app.
2. **Missing legal bases (EN and PL alike).** Privacy Policy §2, "Problem reports": the bullet names no legal basis,
   though every other purpose does (Art. 13(1)(c)). The same gap applies to answering a problem report by e-mail under
   "E-mails".
3. **Firebase Cloud Messaging.** Privacy Policy §4 lists it (a push service) as receiving native crash reports and an
   installation identifier. §2 "Notifications" says nothing is sent from the server. FCM's role is not explained, and a
   lawyer may ask.
4. **"Log" versus "recent activity".** The Privacy Policy's problem-report bullet speaks of "the app's recent log",
   which you can switch off. The app's switch reads "Attach recent activity" (`diagnostics.report.attachLogs`). The
   meaning is the same, but the words differ.
5. **Typography.**
   - support.html PL: „Android 14, Pixel 8" closes with an ASCII `"`.
   - support.html EN uses straight `"` and `'` ("We're", "you're", "Android 14, Pixel 8").
   - company.html EN has "JDG" in straight quotes, unlike the rest of the site's “…” and ’.
6. **Who is the developer.** The index says "Developer: Wiesław Kubasik"; the support and deletion pages say
   "Developer: Kariera Programisty Wiesław Kubasik". This is minor.
7. **Vague wording.** Privacy Policy §2 "Your training": "goals and weekly targets" does not match the app's terms
   (weekly goal, weekly set targets). Rendered generically as *metas y objetivos semanales*.
8. **"Settings → Subscription" names no row** on the Settings screen, whose row reads "Workouts Pro" or "Upgrade". It is
   filed as a fix to the English (LANG-44 in workouts-frontend's `docs/languages-plan.md`). The Spanish kept the path as the English wrote it, with the
   catalogue's `subscriptionPage.title`, *Configuración → Suscripción*, until LANG-44 fixed it.
